iWill privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

iWill ("iWill", "we", "us") is published by CayKen, in the United States. We're responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at onlinemelvin@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your reminders, history, streaks, settings, proof journal and alarm sounds stay on your phone.
  • A photo leaves your phone only when you take one in iWill to check a task (the very first time, only after you confirm a short notice). It goes to our server and to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which tells us whether it shows the task done. We don't keep the photo.
  • You don't create an account with a name or email. iWill uses a random, anonymous ID.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings > Delete my data.

We don't receive your name, email, contacts, location, or payment card details. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Service providers

We use these companies to run iWill. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase (hosting and database; servers in the United States, AWS us-east-2).
  • Anthropic (the Claude AI that checks photos and suggests what to photograph). Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models, and is kept only for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See anthropic.com/legal.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management).
  • Google Play / Apple App Store (app distribution and payments).

Some of these providers are in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • To provide the service you ask for (contract): photo checks, suggestions and Pro.
  • Our legitimate interests: preventing abuse (daily limits) and making checks accurate.
  • Consent, where the law requires it. You can withdraw it by not using photo checks, or by deleting your data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings > Delete my data removes what our server holds (check records, any kept photos, your Pro record and your anonymous account) and everything iWill keeps on your phone. You can also email onlinemelvin@gmail.com, or use https://onlinemelvin.github.io/iwill-legal/delete-data/.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, or to receive a copy of it. Contact us at onlinemelvin@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Deleting your data doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your phone's subscription settings.

Children

iWill isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we don't knowingly process their data.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is protected by access rules, and only our server can read check records.

Test versions

Testers of pre-release versions are told in the app that, during testing, checked photos and reminder names are kept privately to improve the checks. They're deleted before launch.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
