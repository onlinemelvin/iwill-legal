Delete your iWill data

Last updated: November 19, 2026

iWill is published by CayKen. You don't create an account with a name or email: iWill uses a random, anonymous ID, and most of your data never leaves your phone.

Delete everything from the app

Open iWill and go to Settings > Delete my data. This removes, straight away:

  • what our server holds: your photo-check records, any kept photos, your Pro record and your anonymous account;
  • everything iWill keeps on your phone: reminders, history, streaks, settings, proof journal photos and your own alarm sounds.

If you no longer have the app

Uninstalling iWill removes everything it kept on your phone. What's left on our server is tied only to an anonymous ID: photo-check records are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have them removed sooner, email onlinemelvin@gmail.com with "Delete my iWill data" and roughly when you last used photo checks; we'll reply within 30 days.

What isn't deleted

  • Subscriptions: deleting your data doesn't cancel iWill Pro. Cancel it in Google Play or App Store subscription settings.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat, as the stores and the law require.
